一口 OneBite · 隐私政策

本应用不收集任何数据 · This app collects no data

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日（1.1 版起）

一句话版本

「一口」在你的设备上运行，不含任何第三方组件，我们收不到你的任何数据。只有你主动开启 iCloud 同步时，记录才会存到你自己的 iCloud 私有空间，我们同样无法读取。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

无。「一口」不收集、不传输、不共享任何个人信息。

  • 应用没有自己的服务器，不向开发者发送任何数据；
  • 应用不请求相机、麦克风、定位、通讯录等权限。只有你在设置里打开「用餐提醒」时，才会请求通知权限，提醒完全在本机排程；
  • 应用不含统计分析 SDK、广告组件、社交分享组件或任何第三方代码。

2. 数据存储在哪里

你记录的每一餐（口数、咀嚼次数、时间）和应用设置，默认只保存在你设备本地的私有存储区中，不上传到任何我们的服务器。

  • iCloud 同步（默认关闭）：在设置中开启后，已完成的用餐记录会通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 存放在你 Apple 账户的 iCloud 私有数据库中。只有登录同一 Apple 账户的设备能读取，开发者无法访问。关闭同步不会删除任何记录。
  • Apple Watch：在手表上完成的用餐记录会直接传回你配对的 iPhone。

3. 如何删除数据

  • 应用内：在「历史」页面可以随时删除单条记录；
  • 彻底删除：卸载应用即同时删除全部本地数据，且无法恢复；
  • 如果开启过 iCloud 同步，可在 iPhone「设置 › 你的名字 › iCloud › 管理储存空间」中删除「一口」的 iCloud 数据。

4. 儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何年龄段的任何数据，对所有年龄用户（含儿童）同样安全。

5. 政策变更

若未来版本改变数据处理方式，将在此页面更新并同步修订 App Store 隐私标签。

OneBite · Privacy Policy (English)

Effective date: November 20, 2026 (version 1.1 and later)

The short version

OneBite runs on your device and contains no third-party components, so we never receive any of your data. Only if you turn on iCloud sync are your records stored in your own private iCloud space, which we cannot read either.

1. Information we collect

None. OneBite does not collect, transmit to us, or share any personal information. It has no server of its own, requests no camera, microphone, location or contacts access, and contains no analytics SDKs, advertising, or social components. It asks for notification permission only if you turn on meal reminders, which are scheduled on your device.

2. Where data is stored

By default, your meal records and settings are stored only in the app's private on-device storage and are never uploaded to any server of ours. If you turn on iCloud sync (off by default), finished meals are stored in your Apple account's private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit, readable only by your own devices; turning sync off deletes nothing. Meals finished on Apple Watch are sent directly to your paired iPhone.

3. How to delete your data

Delete individual records anytime in the History page, or uninstall the app to remove all local data permanently. If you used iCloud sync, you can also remove OneBite's iCloud data under Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.

4. Children's privacy

The app collects no data from users of any age and is equally safe for children.

5. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated accordingly.
