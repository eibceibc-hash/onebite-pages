一句话版本

「一口」完全在你的设备上运行：不联网、不申请权限、不含任何第三方组件，因此没有也不可能有你的任何数据离开你的手机。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

无。「一口」不收集、不传输、不共享任何个人信息。

  • 应用不访问网络——它在系统层面就没有联网能力；
  • 应用不请求相机、麦克风、定位、通讯录等任何系统权限；
  • 应用不含统计分析 SDK、广告组件、社交分享组件或任何第三方代码。

2. 数据存储在哪里

你记录的每一餐（口数、咀嚼次数、时间）和应用设置，仅保存在你设备本地的私有存储区中。不上传、不同步 iCloud、不备份到任何我们的服务器。

3. 如何删除数据

  • 应用内：在「历史」页面可以随时删除单条记录；
  • 彻底删除：卸载应用即同时删除全部本地数据，且无法恢复。

4. 儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何年龄段的任何数据，对所有年龄用户（含儿童）同样安全。

OneBite · Privacy Policy (English)

Effective date: November 17, 2026

The short version

OneBite runs entirely on your device. It has no internet access, requests no permissions, and contains no third-party components — so no data of yours can ever leave your phone.

1. Information we collect

None. OneBite does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no networking capability, requests no system permissions (camera, microphone, location, contacts, etc.), and contains no analytics SDKs, advertising, or social components.

2. Where data is stored

Your meal records and settings are stored only in the app's private on-device storage. Nothing is uploaded, synced to iCloud, or backed up to any server of ours.

3. How to delete your data

Delete individual records anytime in the History page, or uninstall the app to remove all local data permanently.

4. Children's privacy

The app collects no data from users of any age and is equally safe for children.

5. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated accordingly.
